Tornado Time! sound effect recordings
=====================================
Every recording here is CC0 (public domain dedication) or a US government work in the public domain.
All are free to use in a sold game. They were trimmed, levelled and in some cases layered or re-pitched for the game.

boom.mp3, blast.mp3   Cannon fire. US National Park Service, Natural Sounds gallery. Public domain. nps.gov/subjects/sound
siren.mp3             Siren. US National Park Service, Natural Sounds gallery. Public domain.
plane.wav             Propeller plane. US National Park Service, Natural Sounds gallery. Public domain.

whoosh.mp3            From "Swishes Sound Pack" by artisticdude. CC0. opengameart.org
groan/g1-g24.mp3      "Zombies Sound Pack" by artisticdude. CC0. opengameart.org
scream/y1-y15.mp3     "15 vocal male strain/hurt/pain/jump sounds" by qubodup. CC0. opengameart.org
