# Dead City logo and poster: image prompt

Attach `../logo-dead-city.png` (the current logo) and one or two game screenshots as style references, for example `../shot-ta-6-acid-storm.png` and `../shot-kj-1-atomic-breath.png`.

---

## Main prompt

An epic, cinematic movie-poster key art for a video game called **DEAD CITY**. Vertical poster (2:3), with the title logo across the upper third.

**The tornado (centre, the hero).** A colossal Halloween tornado tearing through a night city. The funnel is near-black and smoky purple, lit from inside by a sickly toxic-green glow. Spinning inside the vortex and flying out of it: glowing carved jack-o'-lanterns with flickering orange faces, swarms of black bats, shattered roof tiles, cars, street signs and splintered trees. Bats also circle the outside of the funnel and scatter toward the camera. Where it touches the ground, a ring of debris and dust is lit orange by fires.

**Lightning (foreground, close to camera).** Jagged ORANGE lightning bolts with dark, almost black edges crack across the very front of the frame, so close they partly overlap the scene, casting orange rim light on everything. More orange forks branch through the storm clouds above.

**The titans (background).** Far behind the tornado, in the smoke of a burning downtown, two gigantic monsters are locked in battle between skyscrapers:
- A charcoal-grey reptilian beast with glowing blue spines along its back, roaring.
- A towering blocky giant robot in red and blue, throwing a punch.

Their silhouettes are lit by explosions and a distant mushroom cloud glowing on the horizon. Buildings collapse around them.

**Destruction everywhere.** Burning cars, collapsing buildings, broken streets with glowing cracks, sparks, flying glass, embers drifting upward, rain streaks, a few fleeing tiny people for scale. A dark, stormy sky in deep purples and greens.

**The logo.** The title **DEAD CITY** in huge, heavy, chiselled block letters, like a blockbuster disaster-movie logo, with:
- a fill grading from toxic neon green at the top to deep purple at the bottom;
- a thick dark-purple bevel and a black drop shadow;
- a glowing green outer edge;
- slight cracks and chips in the letters, with a few bats and sparks crossing them.

A smaller line above it in spaced-out purple caps: **SURVIVE**. Under the logo, two subtitles side by side in green and purple, game-UI style: **TORNADO ALLEY** and **KAIJU MADNESS**. Tagline at the bottom: **BE THE STORM. BE THE MONSTER.**

**Style.** Photoreal, epic blockbuster poster art. Dramatic low camera angle looking up at the tornado, strong depth with a foreground, midground and background, volumetric light, film grain. A rich colour palette of purple, toxic green and hot Halloween orange against near-black. Sharp detail, high contrast, 4K. Keep it playful and spooky Halloween rather than gory.

**Avoid:** real copyrighted monsters or robots (no Godzilla, King Kong, Transformers or Gundam; use the original designs described above), real brand logos, gore, extra or misspelled text, watermarks.

## Variations

- **Logo only (for the itch header or the banner).** *"The words DEAD CITY in huge, heavy, chiselled block letters, toxic neon-green to deep-purple gradient fill, dark-purple bevel, black drop shadow, glowing green rim, slightly cracked. A small orange-lit Halloween tornado full of jack-o'-lanterns and bats twisting up behind the letters. Orange lightning arcing across. Transparent or plain black background, centred, wide 3:1."*
- **Wide banner (960 × 400 or 960 × 200).** Use the main prompt but with a wide 16:9 or 3:1 frame: the tornado on the left, the two titans fighting on the right, and the logo centred over the middle.
- **Square cover (630 × 500).** Use the main prompt cropped tight on the tornado, with the logo across the bottom third.

## Tips

- If the text comes out garbled, generate the art without text ("no text") and add the **DEAD CITY** logo afterwards. I can lay the title over the art in the same style as our current logo.
- Ask for 3–4 versions and pick the one where the tornado and titans read clearly at small sizes. On itch, the cover is shown about 315 px wide.
