# Dead City commercial: video prompt

Paste the master prompt into the video generator. Attach `gameplay-tornado-alley.mp4` and `gameplay-kaiju-madness.mp4` from this folder as the gameplay references. Most generators make 5–10 second shots, so the shot list below is also split into individual prompts you can generate one at a time and cut together.

## Master prompt

A 45-second, epic, cinematic trailer for a free browser game called **Dead City**. It is in two parts: **Tornado Alley**, where you play as a living tornado wrecking a city, and the bonus game **Kaiju Madness**, where you play as a giant monster.

**Style:** photorealistic, blockbuster disaster-movie look. Shot on large-format cinema cameras, anamorphic lens flares, dramatic storm lighting, volumetric dust and rain, slow-motion debris, deep bass booms and rumbling thunder. The colour grade is dark teal storm skies with hot orange explosions, and a sickly neon-green glow from the tornado. The pacing builds steadily to a huge climax.

**How the game works (show this, don't explain it):** You ARE the storm. Tap or drag to steer the tornado through town. It sucks up cars, people, trees and whole houses, and gets bigger with everything it eats, growing from an F1 twister into a city-swallowing F5. Tap anywhere and a bolt of lightning strikes there first. Wreck landmarks such as the power plant (citywide blackout), the water tower and the nuclear plant, which goes up in a mushroom cloud and turns your storm into a glowing yellow NUKULAR NADO. Seeding planes try to spray you weaker, and storm chasers try to photograph you. In Kaiju Madness you are a giant monster: eat people to grow from Hatchling to Titan to Colossus, flatten skyscrapers, fight rival monsters in hand-to-hand brawls, and grab MEGA boosters that set off a nuke around you.

### Shot list

**1. Cold open (0:00–0:04).** Black screen. Distant thunder. Lightning flashes reveal an American downtown at dusk under a churning, rotating supercell. Emergency sirens wail. A slow push in on empty streets, with newspapers blowing and traffic lights swinging.

**2. The storm forms (0:04–0:09).** A colossal tornado drops out of the clouds. Its funnel glows with an eerie toxic-green light from within, and green lightning crawls along it. Low-angle hero shot from street level as it touches down and tears up the road. Cars lift and tumble upward into the vortex.

**3. Title card (0:09–0:11).** The funnel fills the frame. Smash cut to the title in huge, rough, glowing neon-green letters, with debris whipping past it and a thunder crack on the cut: **BE THE STORM.**

**4. Destruction montage (0:11–0:20).** Fast cuts, a mix of slow motion and real time:
- A suburban house ripped off its foundation, roof first.
- A water tower buckling and bursting.
- A power plant exploding in a shower of sparks, so the whole city skyline goes dark block by block.
- Lightning striking a gas station, which erupts in a fireball.
- A small propeller plane trying to spray the storm, then being sucked in.
- A storm chaser's camera flash going off, before the chaser's truck is flung away.

**5. The nuke (0:20–0:24).** The tornado tears through a nuclear power plant. Blinding white flash. A mushroom cloud rises in the background behind the skyline while a shockwave rolls across the city, flattening buildings in a ring. Silence for half a second, then a massive boom. Through the dust, the tornado re-emerges glowing bright radioactive yellow, bigger than ever.

**6. Real to game (0:24–0:28).** One continuous camera move. The camera cranes up and back from the yellow tornado, rising high over the city into an overhead, angled, isometric view. As it rises, the photoreal city smoothly morphs into stylised low-poly 3D game graphics: the same streets, the same tornado and the same destruction, now in the game's chunky blocky look. No hard cut; the real world melts into the game. Match the angle and framing of the attached Tornado Alley gameplay so the transition lands exactly on it.

**7. Gameplay (0:28–0:34).** Real Tornado Alley gameplay from the attached clip: the storm growing as it chews through town, lightning taps, the nuclear blast and the yellow Nukular Nado. On-screen text: **TAP TO STEER. EAT THE TOWN. GROW TO F5.**

**8. Kaiju tease (0:34–0:39).** Hard cut back to photorealism. A ruined city at night, fires everywhere, the dust still glowing from the nuke on the horizon. Two colossal monsters charge toward each other down a wide avenue from opposite ends:
- A charcoal-grey, Godzilla-like beast with glowing blue back plates.
- A giant ape.

Each step cracks the asphalt and shatters windows. Cars bounce. Cut between their faces and their feet, faster and faster. Just before they collide, freeze for a split second on the moment of impact, then a white flash.

**9. Bonus game reveal (0:39–0:42).** Out of the flash, the monsters snap into the game's low-poly style. Same moment, same angle, matching the attached Kaiju Madness gameplay: the brawl, Spin & Throw, and the MEGA nuke mushroom cloud. Title card in bold, chunky, neon-green letters: **PLUS THE BONUS GAME: KAIJU MADNESS.** Second line: **40 MONSTERS. ONE CITY. NO MERCY.**

**10. End card (0:42–0:45).** The **DEAD CITY** logo over a slow push on a smoking ruined skyline with a distant tornado. Text:
- **PLAY FREE NOW**
- **devs66.itch.io/survive-dead-city**

One last rumble of thunder.

**Audio:** epic orchestral trailer music with heavy percussion and taiko drums. Bass drops on the title cards, and complete silence just before the nuke flash and before the monster collision.

**Avoid:** real brand logos, real copyrighted monsters or characters (the monsters must be original designs, not Godzilla or King Kong), gore, and readable real company names.
