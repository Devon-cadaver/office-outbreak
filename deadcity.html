DEAD CITY
NIGHTS

Survive six nights. About 20 slow zombies the first night, 60% more each night after, and they get faster. Lead survivors to the green zone and a chopper lifts them out. Spend your cash at dawn. After night six, the chopper comes for you.

Left thumb: joystick. Drag the right side to turn the camera. Guns aim and fire on their own; FIRE throws by hand. Double-tap next to a vehicle to drive it, double-tap again to get out. Vehicles burn fuel and take hits; refuel at the gas station. The tank needs its keys and fires shells from the FIRE button.
