Tap or drag on the grid to paint the selected item. A new map is all buildings: paint roads through it. Curves, T-junctions and crossroads come from how road cells touch, and the grid shows the exact shape. The outer ring stays buildings. Streets: Road, Avenue (wider, crossings at junctions), Crosswalk (stripes on every side), Plaza (open pavement, no road), Parked car. Buildings block the way; Tower is a skyscraper. Park: Grass is walkable, Tree blocks, Bench sits at the edge of a grass cell. Place one Start and one Exit, then press Play. The map is saved in this browser.
